Stand Firm: Preparing for the Test

No one becomes a martyr overnight. The strength to refuse the mark on the last day is built on all the ordinary days before it.

Home › Stand Firm

Fr. Charles Arminjon wrote that God announced this trial in advance so that we might “prepare ourselves even now to sustain it,” by turning to him in prayer and taking up “the spiritual weapons of charity and faith” (The End of the Present World, p. 48). These five pages are written for that purpose. They stand on their own: a reader who does not share Fr. John's reading of current events should still find in them a sound and hopeful preparation for any trial of faith.

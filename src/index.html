Where the book begins

“The revelation of Jesus Christ, which God gave to him, to show his servants what must happen soon. He made it known by sending his angel to his servant John, who gives witness to the word of God and to the testimony of Jesus Christ by reporting what he saw. Blessed is the one who reads aloud and blessed are those who listen to this prophetic message and heed what is written in it, for the appointed time is near.”Revelation 1:1–3

Bosch paints John on Patmos looking up from his book to the Woman clothed with the sun, the great sign of Revelation 12, with an angel at his side. That is the posture this site asks of its readers: to read, to listen, and to look up.

Welcome Message

The past ten years should give us pause to take a focused look at the Book of Revelation, in particular chapters 12 and 13. The political events, especially in the United States of America, and the technological advances around the world have prompted alarm and confusion. People everywhere are trying to make sense of it all. And yet, shouldn't we be expecting this sudden chain of events?

Please take some time to (re)read these two chapters along with the many sacred scripture passages, Marian apparitions and witness statements that you will find on this and other websites I link to, which prepare us for this moment in the history of salvation. Our Lord Jesus Christ is always present to guide and protect our journey towards eternal life.

In the Book of Genesis, on the cross in the Gospel of John, and in the Apocalypse, the Word of God reveals to us that Mary, the mother of Jesus who is God, is also our Blessed Mother. She is God's gift to us, particularly in this time of trial and tribulation. As the Woman clothed with the sun, may she intercede for us and help us to see clearly what we must do by the grace of God to remain faithful to our commitment to love, worship, serve and adore the Most Holy Trinity, God the Father, Son and Holy Spirit.

Fr. John

Where this site standsFr. John proposes, as a possibility to be tested and not as the Church's teaching, that the first beast of Revelation 13 may be fulfilled in our time in Donald J. Trump. He grades this possible, never settled, and the Church has not spoken on it. The site sets no date, names no one as the final Antichrist, and invites no one to act against anyone. Faithful Catholics disagree with this reading; they are not marked, and they are not our enemies. What follows is offered so that readers can test the reading for themselves, and above all so that every reader, whatever they conclude, is ready to refuse the worship that belongs to God alone.

Hope: the Woman clothed with the sun

Revelation 12 opens with “a great sign”: a woman clothed with the sun, with the moon under her feet, and on her head a crown of twelve stars (Revelation 12:1; see also agreatsign.si). The dragon makes war on her and on her children, but she is carried to safety and cared for in the desert, and the dragon is conquered “by the blood of the Lamb and by the word of their testimony” (Revelation 12:11; see thefirstbeast.com for more on the dragon).

To read the whole book with the Church, chapter by chapter, you are invited to The Book of Revelation · A Catholic Study at thelambsvictory.org.

Fr. John's reflections on Revelation 12, verse by verse, and pages on Mary and the saints in time of tribulation are coming to this site.

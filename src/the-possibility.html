The Possibility: My Proposal

How I read Revelation 13 in our time, mark by mark, with the evidence, the grades, and the case against.

Home › The Possibility

These pages set out my proposal: that the beasts of Revelation 13 may be fulfilled in our time. I offer it as a possibility to be tested, not as the Church's teaching. Each mark is graded possible or probable, never settled, and each is set beside the strongest case against it. No date is set, no one is named as the final Antichrist, and words and deeds are weighed, never souls (CCC 1861). If you are new to this site, begin with Watch, which explains how I read and why.

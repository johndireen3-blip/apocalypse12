As I wrote in my Welcome Message, the past ten years should give us pause to take a focused look at the Book of Revelation, and in particular at chapters 12 and 13. Political events, especially in the United States, and technological advances around the world have prompted alarm and confusion. People everywhere are trying to make sense of it all. And yet, shouldn't we have expected this sudden chain of events?

I have shared my thoughts online on various websites over the years. This website grew out of an earlier one, where I stated my proposal plainly but without critical review. Below is what I wrote there, beginning in August 2023, with the updates I added over the following years.

The next page, The Evidence, is where the critical review begins, with the help of Claude, the AI model made by Anthropic (see its own page, How Claude Reads Revelation 13). On Apocalypse 13, my proposal is weighed by the same standards as everyone else's and set side by side with other proposals, such as Peter Thiel's.

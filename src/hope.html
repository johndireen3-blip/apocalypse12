Revelation 13 is not the whole story, and it is not the first part of it. Before the beasts rise, St. John sees a woman clothed with the sun, and before the trial begins, God has already prepared a place for her. These pages are the hope that holds everything else on this site: the Woman, her Son's victory, and the help God gives us for the struggle.

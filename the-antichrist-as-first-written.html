The First Beast is The Antichrist

“Then I saw a beast come out of the sea with ten horns and seven heads; on its horns were ten diadems, and on its heads blasphemous name[s]” (Rev. 13:1).

Days after the bombing of Hiroshima and Nagasaki in Japan, which perhaps opened a portal to Hell and released the Spirit of The Antichrist into our world, Donald J. Trump was conceived (b. June 14, 1946). “‘He will be a child of fornication,’ says St. John Damascene, ‘and his birth will be saturated with the breath and spirit of Satan’.” (End of the Present World (EPW) by Fr. Charles Arminjon, p. 43.)

The First Beast, the Antichrist, rises from the Sea

The sea represents people of the world, and the beast, which reveals itself over seven different time periods (or hills, as in City Upon A Hill), each led by its own head, is of the people, who becomes a powerful ruler (see Rev. 17:9 and Dn. 7:17). The last head reigns twice. “The beast that existed once but exists no longer is an eighth king, but really belongs to the seven and is headed for destruction. The ten horns that you saw represent ten kings (diadems) who have not yet been crowned” (Rev. 17:11-12). Each head is blasphemous, as he presents himself above God.

Description of the First Beast, the Antichrist

“The beast I saw was like a leopard, but it had feet like a bear’s, and its mouth was like the mouth of a lion. To it the dragon gave its own power and throne, along with great authority” (Rev. 13:2).

Each of these seven heads has its own characteristics, which altogether describe the one beast. The Book of Daniel 7:1-6 describes the beastly characteristics of the lion, bear and leopard, which the apostle John refers to in his vision. The Prophet Daniel received a vision of four beasts arising from the same sea of evil, while St. John received a similar vision, but as one beast with seven heads. The dragon is Satan, aka Lucifer, the fallen angel. He gives his power and throne to the First Beast, the Antichrist, Donald J. Trump.

The throne of Satan and the First Beast, the Antichrist

“Then war broke out in heaven; Michael and his angels battled against the dragon. The dragon and its angels fought back, but they did not prevail and there was no longer any place for them in heaven. The huge dragon, the ancient serpent, who is called the Devil and Satan, who deceived the whole world, was thrown down to earth, and its angels were thrown down with it” (Rev. 12:7-9).

Satan cannot become incarnate, the way the Son of God, Our Lord Jesus Christ became human. However, he can give his “power and throne, along with great authority” to anyone he wishes. He attempted to do so in the third temptation he proposed to Christ:

“Then the devil took him up to a very high mountain, and showed him all the kingdoms of the world in their magnificence, and he said to him, ‘All these I shall give to you, if you will prostrate yourself and worship me.’ At this, Jesus said to him, ‘Get away, Satan! It is written: ‘The Lord, your God, shall you worship and him alone shall you serve’’” (Mt. 4:8-10).

The Devil gives his throne to the First Beast, the Antichrist

Jesus rejected all of Satan’s efforts to corrupt him, but the First Beast, the Antichrist (all seven heads), embody this last temptation. Together, these seven heads, as one beast, reign over the world to dominate and destroy its inhabitants. The 7th & 8th heads belong to one person, Donald J. Trump.

“I saw that one of its heads seemed to have been mortally wounded, but this mortal wound was healed. Fascinated, the whole world followed after the beast” (Rev. 13:3).

Whether former President Donald J. Trump lost the 2020 election, or it was stolen, in either case he lost his power. It appears to be a mortal wound to the Head of State. However, in spite of the 4 indictments, encompassing 91 charges, which he must face in court, there is a real possibility he will be reelected. This explains the 8th king, who is really one of the seven, and it is where we find ourselves at the present time (August 2023).

As of the end of September, 2023, there have been two Republican primary debates. It has become clear to some that Donald J. Trump is not mortally wounded, but well on his way to returning to power. Newt: The race is over.

He is Arrogant and Condescending

Vote, then die. This beast is all about himself. In spite of such arrogant indifference to the well-being of others, he won the Iowa caucus with 51% of the vote January 15th, 2024. His condescending statement to the voters of Iowa is in reality a warning to all, vote for him and then die. The day after the March 5th “Super Tuesday” Trump became the only candidate for the republican nomination.

The First Beast, the Antichrist, is the Lawless One

May 30, 2024, Trump was convicted of all 34 counts in just one of his four inditements charged against him. He is now a convicted felon. Add this to the list of crimes attributed to Trump, and we can see why St. Paul calls him “the Lawless One” (2 Thes. 2). This conviction and his life of lawlessness should open our eyes to who this person is.

November 5, 2024 Donald J. Trump is elected 47th President of the United States of America. This fulfills the event foretold in chapter 13, verse 3, of the Book of Revelation. People around the world are amazed that he has been reelected, and are following him with fascination. Time Magazine names him 2024 Person of the Year.

The Prophet Daniel describes the First Beast, the Antichrist

“After this, in the visions of the night I saw a fourth beast, terrifying, horrible, and of extraordinary strength; it had great iron teeth with which it devoured and crushed, and it trampled with its feet what was left. It differed from the beasts that preceded it. It had ten horns. I was considering the ten horns it had, when suddenly another, a little horn, sprang out of their midst, and three of the previous horns were torn away to make room for it. This horn had eyes like human eyes, and a mouth that spoke arrogantly” (Dn. 7:7-8)

Specific outstanding characteristics are given in Sacred Scripture to help us recognize the First Beast, the Antichrist, when we see him. Yet, many people continue to support him. There is nothing normal about this! There is only one explanation for this as we read in Sacred Scripture:

“They worshiped the dragon because it gave its authority to the beast; they also worshiped the beast and said, ‘Who can compare with the beast or who can fight against it?’” (Rev. 13:4)

Reject Donald J. Trump. Do not follow him.

Do not worship the dragon, Satan, and do not worship the beast, Donald J. Trump, even if we cannot fight against this evil one! Once he is sworn in on inauguration day, January 20, 2025, his time will be short to accomplish all this beast must do for our ruin.

“The beast was given a mouth uttering proud boasts and blasphemies, and it was given authority to act for forty-two months.” (Rev. 13:5)

The Book of the Prophet Daniel and the Book of Revelation provide insight about the end times. We can only understand it as we live through the time of Tribulation with the help of Our Lady of Apocalypse. God said:

“Go, Daniel,” he said, “because the words are to be kept secret and sealed until the end time. Many shall be refined, purified, and tested, but the wicked shall prove wicked; the wicked shall have no understanding, but those with insight shall. From the time that the daily sacrifice is abolished and the desolating abomination is set up, there shall be one thousand two hundred and ninety days. Blessed are they who have patience and persevere for the one thousand three hundred and thirty-five days. Go, take your rest, you shall rise for your reward at the end of days.” (Dn 12:9-13)

Former and future President Donald Trump is about to resume the clock on the 7-year tribulation. It is likely that his first term in office completed four years of this time frame and that inauguration day (Jan 20, 2025) begins the last three years.

The Woman & the Dragon

Once we are put to the test, there will not be much time to think. The choice Revelation sets before us has to be understood before it is asked of us. This site is a priest's attempt to help you understand it now, and to stand firm when it comes.

“Here is what sustains the holy ones who keep God's commandments and their faith in Jesus.”Revelation 14:12
Stand firmThe evidence

Where this site standsFr. John proposes, as a possibility to be tested and not as the Church's teaching, that the beast of Revelation 13 may be fulfilled in our time in Donald J. Trump. He grades this possible, never settled, and the Church has not spoken on it. The site sets no date, names no one as the final Antichrist, and invites no one to act against anyone. Faithful Catholics disagree with this reading; they are not marked, and they are not our enemies. What follows is offered so that readers can test the reading for themselves, and above all so that every reader, whatever they conclude, is ready to refuse the worship that belongs to God alone.

Hope: the Woman clothed with the sun

Revelation 12 opens with “a great sign”: a woman clothed with the sun, with the moon under her feet, and on her head a crown of twelve stars (Revelation 12:1). The dragon makes war on her and on her children, but she is carried to safety and cared for in the desert, and the dragon is conquered “by the blood of the Lamb and by the word of their testimony” (Revelation 12:11). Fr. John's reflections on Revelation 12, verse by verse, and pages on Mary and the saints in time of tribulation are coming to this site.

Watch: Why Scripture Asks Us to Stay Awake

“What I say to you, I say to all: ‘Watch!’” (Mark 13:37). Christ did not give this command to a few specialists in prophecy. He gave it to every disciple.

These three pages lay the foundation for everything else on this site. Like the pages of Stand Firm, they stand on their own: a reader who does not share my reading of current events should still find in them what the Church has always taught about keeping watch.
